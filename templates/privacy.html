Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly, such as your account details (username, email) and financial transaction records (expense amounts, categories, dates, and optional notes) that you enter to track your expenses.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide, maintain, and improve the Spendly personal expense tracking experience. This includes displaying your category breakdowns, calculating monthly summaries, and securing your account.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal and financial information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your data remains private to your account.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We may use trusted standard web services (such as font providers and hosting infrastructure) necessary to operate the application.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to us through the project repository or support channels.
